Route Scout

Compare every integrated Stellar anchor by payout, fees, speed, and verified on-chain settlement reliability — before routing a payment.

Disclosure — where our interests sit. Landfall grades anchors from the public ledger, and this same page also quotes and executes crypto swaps through NEAR Intents. That is a conflict worth naming: a tool that both rates routes and carries traffic has an interest in which route you pick. Concretely, today — reliability grades are computed only from ledger activity and never from whether an anchor is a commercial partner; Landfall takes no fee, referral, or rebate from any anchor listed here; the fee and rate figures in the anchor table are each anchor's own published terms, not something we are paid to present; and no anchor can pay to appear, rank higher, or be removed. Landfall never holds your funds in either flow. If any of that changes, this notice changes with it.

Move it

One control for the whole journey. Pick what you are sending and where it should end up — another chain, or cash in a local currency — and the route is worked out for you. Crypto legs are live quotes from NEAR Intents; the cash leg goes through a Stellar anchor's own regulated flow. Connect a wallet once and it stays connected across both. Landfall never holds your funds at any point in either leg.

🔀 Pick a destination chain and click Get Live Quote.

Send it for real

Non-custodial — you sign with your own wallet (Freighter or Lobstr for a Stellar send; MetaMask, Trust Wallet, or any other browser wallet that supports EIP-6963 for an EVM chain send) and Landfall never holds your funds or your key. Experimental: double-check the destination address and amount before signing — a sent transaction cannot be reversed.
