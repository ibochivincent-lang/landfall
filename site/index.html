What a low return rate does not mean

A return is the honest failure mode. When an anchor can't complete the off-chain leg of a withdrawal, sending the asset back is what a good actor does — and that return is visible on the ledger.

An anchor that accepts value, fails to settle, and simply keeps it produces no return event at all. It scores a clean 0.00% — identical to an anchor that never fails. The worst conduct we could hope to detect is exactly the conduct that leaves no trace in this metric.

So 0.13% is not a clean bill of health. It is the absence of one specific kind of evidence. Closing that gap needs the fiat leg, which means signed settlement attestations — the next thing we're building.
