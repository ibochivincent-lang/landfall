# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Landfall to be a project anyone can contribute to, regardless of
experience level, background, nationality, or identity. Contributors come to
this project from many countries and many levels of experience; a first pull
request from someone new is as welcome as a fix from a maintainer.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about code and kind about people. Criticise the change, not the person.
- Assume good faith. Most disagreements are missing context, not bad intent.
- Explain your reasoning in reviews. "This is wrong" helps nobody; "this breaks
  when the account has no payment history, see the test" helps everyone.
- English is a second language for many contributors here. Read for meaning,
  not for grammar.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or imagery of any kind
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews
- Claiming credit for work you did not do

## A note specific to this project

Landfall publishes findings about real businesses. Discussions about a specific
anchor must stay factual and evidence-based. **Do not use project channels to
accuse an anchor of misconduct.** If the ledger shows an account has been
dormant for 40 days, say that; do not extrapolate to fraud. Our credibility
depends on the distinction between what we measured and what we suspect.

Findings are disputed by re-checking the ledger, not by argument.

## Reporting

Report anything that breaches this document by opening a private security
advisory on the repository, or by contacting the maintainers directly. Reports
are handled confidentially. Maintainers who do not follow this code may face
the same consequences as anyone else.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or delete contributions that breach this document,
and may temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behaviour they judge
inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
